##Problem

Payment webhooks from the provider fail intermittently under load. Failed deliveries are dropped rather than retried, so a customer's subscription silently lapses.

##Requirements

The retry worker MUST re-attempt any delivery that returns a retryable status, backing off exponentially between attempts, and MUST stop after five attempts or 24 hours, whichever comes first.

Deliveries must be processed in order where the provider supplies a sequence number. Ordering is not guaranteed across partitions.

  • Deliveries are idempotent — the consumer MUST tolerate duplicates.
  • Each attempt is recorded with attempt number, timestamp and response code.
